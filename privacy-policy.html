KorschSales – Privacy Policy Last updated: June 5, 2026 KorschSales is an internal sales tool developed by and for Korsch America Inc. This app is distributed exclusively to authorized KORSCH employees and sales representatives via Apple Business Manager. It is not available to the general public. 1. Information We Collect KorschSales does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data entered or generated within the app remains on your device. The app stores the following data locally on your device only: CPO (Certified Pre-Owned) equipment inventory data A hashed Inventory Admin PIN (used only by designated administrators) User preferences and app settings 2. Inventory Synchronization Authorized administrators may publish CPO inventory updates to a private, access-controlled repository. This data contains only equipment listings (machine models, specifications, and pricing). No personal user data is ever uploaded or synchronized. 3. Data Sharing Korsch America Inc does not sell, trade, or share any data collected by this app with third parties. The app does not include any advertising SDKs, analytics services, or third-party tracking tools. 4. Data Security KorschSales does not require a login or personal authentication to use. The Inventory Admin PIN, used only by designated administrators, is stored exclusively as a cryptographic hash and cannot be reversed. We recommend keeping your device updated with the latest iOS security patches. 5. Children’s Privacy KorschSales is intended solely for use by Korsch America Inc employees and business partners. It is not directed at children under the age of 13. 6. Changes to This Policy Korsch America Inc may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any revised policy. 7.
